🎯 Mission & Vision

Mission: Deliver secure, transparent, and scalable clan governance infrastructure.

Vision: Become the global standard for digital community governance systems.

⚙️ How It Works

  1. Secure member onboarding
  2. RBAC role assignment with inheritance
  3. Controlled module access
  4. Immutable audit logging
  5. Governance workflows (votes, approvals)
  6. Reporting & analytics

🧩 Core Modules

  • Identity: Auth, roles, lifecycle control
  • Financials: Ledger, loans, fines
  • Genealogy: Tree + lineage engine
  • Governance: Voting + compliance
  • Events: Scheduling + attendance
  • Communication: Feed + SMS alerts

🔐 Security & Compliance

  • RBAC + Least Privilege
  • Separation of Duties (SoD)
  • Immutable Audit Logs
  • CSRF / XSS Protection
  • Rate Limiting
  • Encryption (in transit & at rest)

Aligned with ISO 27001 & SOC2 principles
